Introducing ProSale v2: Built for Modern Estate Sales

A New Era for Estate Sale Management Begins

In today’s fast-moving estate sale industry, efficiency, accuracy, and scalability aren’t just goals—they’re necessities. At Rivendell AI Consulting and Contracting, we recognized these challenges firsthand. That’s why our team has fully rebuilt ProSale v2 from the ground up—a platform engineered entirely in-house to address the core pain points of estate sale professionals.

This ambitious overhaul was developed by our internal engineering and design teams, leveraging deep domain expertise to create a unified system that handles inventory, pricing, reporting, and payments—all from one centralized platform. Unlike patchwork solutions or incremental updates, ProSale v2 represents a complete transformation built to eliminate inefficiencies like double entry, manual reconciliation, and disconnected workflows.

You’ll learn how our proprietary technology enables cloud-based dashboards, automated marketing, and mobile-first check-in systems that reduce lines and improve buyer satisfaction. Discover how our platform supports compliance with local sales tax regulations in states like Texas, Colorado, and Georgia, while enabling seamless collaboration among teams, appraisers, and vendors.

Whether managing single-home liquidations or large-scale events, ProSale v2 delivers the performance and reliability estate professionals need to grow their businesses in 2026 and beyond.

Built by RivendellAI: A Platform Engineered for Real-World Results

ProSale v2 isn’t the work of an offshore contractor or a generic software template. It’s the result of focused innovation by our team at Rivendell AI Consulting and Contracting—professionals who understand the operational demands of estate sales because we’ve built solutions for them from day one.

Every feature in this platform was designed and developed in-house, ensuring tight integration and true ownership of the codebase. Our engineers, data scientists, and UX designers collaborated closely to create a system that doesn’t just look modern, but performs like one—delivering real-time synchronization, offline mobile functionality, and end-to-end automation.

This level of control allows us to innovate faster, respond to user feedback, and maintain security and stability across every component. From the AI-driven cataloging engine to the document generator, each module reflects our commitment to solving the tedious, time-consuming aspects of estate management so professionals can focus on client relationships and business growth.

Unified Online and In-Person Sales: One Catalog, One System

The biggest inefficiency in traditional estate sales? Running separate systems for online and in-person events. That leads to double entry, pricing discrepancies, and reporting errors. ProSale v2 eliminates that friction by running both channels on a single, synchronized catalog—all developed and maintained by our team at Rivendell AI Consulting and Contracting.

Buyers can now browse and purchase items directly online, with holds, checkout, and settlement all handled end-to-end—while in-person shoppers at the physical estate sale see the same inventory and pricing in real time. When an item sells online, it’s instantly marked as sold on-site, and vice versa. This unified approach ensures consistency across channels, reduces labor, and maximizes exposure. No more spreadsheets or last-minute inventory scrambles.

For estate professionals, this means faster reconciliation, fewer disputes, and cleaner reporting. Clients get clarity and transparency throughout the process. Whether you’re downsizing in Seattle or liquidating a high-value estate in Atlanta, ProSale v2 ensures every sale—online or in-person—is seamless, accurate, and professional.

AI That Does the Tedious Work

Let’s face it: cataloging and pricing thousands of items is a time-consuming, often tedious part of the estate sale process. ProSale v2 is where RivendellAI earns its name—by embedding intelligence directly into the workflow to eliminate manual labor.

AI photo tagging allows crews to snap a picture of an item and receive instant suggestions for what it is—a vintage lamp, a mid-century chair, a set of silverware. This dramatically speeds up entry and reduces data-entry fatigue.

AI comparables tackle the hardest part of pricing: judgment. Instead of relying on gut feeling or outdated reference books, ProSale v2 surfaces recent sales data for similar items in your local market. This helps sellers price with confidence—not guesswork—improving buyer trust and maximizing returns.

These aren’t bolted-on gimmicks. They’re woven into the core cataloging and pricing flow, saving hours per event and helping teams focus on higher-value tasks like client communication and event strategy.

Reports and Documents That Build Trust

Every estate sale generates paperwork—settlement statements, consignment reports, contracts, and tax summaries. Too often, these documents are thrown together at the end from disorganized spreadsheets, harming professionalism and client trust.

ProSale v2 changes that with a dedicated document and report generator that produces clean, branded PDFs on demand. Want to hand your client a polished settlement statement minutes after closing? Now you can. Need a breakdown of consignment percentages, fees, and net payouts? Generate it in one click.

These documents aren’t just functional—they’re part of your brand. With professional formatting, your business looks more credible, organized, and trustworthy. That level of polish can be the difference between a satisfied client and a referral.

Mobile-First Inventory Management: From the Floor to the Cloud

Cataloging happens on the ground, not at a desk. That’s why ProSale v2 includes a mobile inventory app purpose-built for estate sale crews.

With this app, teams can photograph, tag, price, and print labels right at the point of discovery—no walking back and forth to a laptop. Integrated barcode scanning and thermal label printing ensure every item is consistently tracked from catalog to checkout.

And because it’s fully synced with the cloud-based system, what the crew captures on the floor goes live instantly—whether that’s online buyers browsing from afar or staff checking inventory on-site. Offline mode ensures functionality even in low-signal areas like basements or closets.

This mobile-first approach cuts cataloging time in half and keeps your team focused where they belong: on-site and efficient.

Flexible Pricing and Self-Service Growth

We rebuilt the business model to match how estate sale companies actually grow. ProSale v2 offers subscription tiers with per-sale add-ons, so you only pay for what you need. Need extra support for a large estate weekend? Add capacity seamlessly.

For resellers and franchise operators, we’ve built self-service franchise enrollment, so new teams can sign up, onboard, and get provisioned without waiting on support. This enables faster scaling and reduces administrative overhead—critical for growing businesses.

Payments That Fit the Way You Work

Handling payments securely and professionally is a top priority. ProSale v2 integrates with Square today for seamless credit and contactless payments, both online and in-person.

The architecture is designed to support multiple payment processors—so whether you use Stripe, PayPal, or another provider, the system adapts to your needs. ACH payouts and Stripe Connect are on the near-term roadmap, enabling automated disbursements to sellers, staff, and vendors without manual transfers or checks.

This end-to-end payment flow ensures money moves cleanly from buyer to business to client—reducing risk, improving cash flow, and saving hours of reconciliation.

The Road Ahead

ProSale v2 is live—fully operational and already transforming how estate sales are run. But this isn’t the final product; it’s the foundation.

We’ve cleared the runway to build what’s next: deeper AI tooling for automated inventory categorization, richer marketplace integrations to expand buyer reach, and fully automated payouts that close the loop on settlements.

The estate sale industry is evolving. With ProSale v2, you’re not just keeping up—you’re leading the change.
