Modernizing Prosale: A New Chapter in Estate Sales Software

At RivendellAI Inc, we recently took over the development and modernization of www.Prosale.com — a comprehensive Estate Sales Management system that helps professionals manage auctions, inventory, invoicing, and customer engagement.

Prosale was originally built with Angular 8 on the frontend and a .NET backend — a solid foundation, but one in need of modernization. Our team has embarked on a significant effort to bring the system up to date, starting with:

  • 🚀 Upgrading Angular from v8 to the latest version, a challenging but necessary step to improve security, performance, and long-term maintainability.

  • 🧩 Adding a new consignment subsystem, designed as a modern microservice using Quarkus (for high performance on the JVM) and Qute for the UI layer.

  • 🔗 Integrating Quarkus with the existing .NET backend, so new features can evolve independently without disrupting core Prosale workflows.

This hybrid architecture gives us flexibility: we can move fast with new services like consignment, while carefully upgrading and maintaining the legacy Angular/.NET stack.

🧠 Looking Ahead: AI and LLM Integration

While our current focus is on modernizing the tech stack and extending functionality, the long-term vision goes far beyond UI upgrades and system refactoring.

At RivendellAI, we’re preparing Prosale for a future powered by generative AI.

Our goal is to integrate a Large Language Model (LLM) — such as LLaMA 3 — to provide natural language access to Prosale’s extensive datasets, including:

  • Inventory and sales history

  • Client records and consignment agreement

  • Scanned documents, photos, and estate item images

Imagine an estate sales manager being able to ask:

“Show me all items consigned by John Smith in the past year over $500.” or “Generate a marketing summary for this weekend’s sale with photos and item highlights.”

This is where enterprise-grade AI meets real business need — and it’s the next chapter we’re building toward.
