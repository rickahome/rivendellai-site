Flagship platform · ProSale

A complete estate-sale, retail & consignment platform

A full commerce operating system for estate-sale and resale businesses — point-of-sale, inventory, online storefronts, consignor management, shipping, reporting, and companion mobile apps for staff and shoppers. Designed, built, and operated end to end.

  • ~18 .NET microservices behind an API gateway on AWS (Kubernetes/EKS), one unified database, Redis infrastructure.
  • Card-present POS with Square, cash drawers & registers, cross-sale checkout, receipts and refunds.
  • Live carrier shipping — real-time rates and label purchase across USPS/UPS/FedEx, with a fulfillment queue.
  • Public online storefronts, SEO catalog, multi-vendor carts and online payments with per-seller payouts.
  • Subscription billing & self-service company signup (pay-before-provision), tiered feature entitlements.
  • Companion mobile apps (iOS + Android from one codebase) for operators and member shoppers.
  • Automated data migration moving an entire legacy business from Azure to AWS, one company at a time.
